☕ Classic Hot Coffee Drinks
Drink	Description
Espresso	A strong shot of coffee made by forcing hot water through finely ground beans.
Americano	Espresso + hot water (lighter than espresso but still strong).
Latte	Espresso + steamed milk + a thin layer of milk foam.
Cappuccino	Equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and thick foam.
Flat White	Similar to a latte but with more espresso and less milk.
Macchiato	Espresso “stained” with a little milk or foam.
Mocha	Espresso + steamed milk + chocolate syrup or cocoa + whipped cream.
Café au Lait	Brewed coffee + steamed milk (French-style).
